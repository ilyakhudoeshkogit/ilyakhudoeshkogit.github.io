An intranet portal on SharePoint Online: department sites, news, requests, booking and reports. One SPFx solution, versions 1.0 → 1.6.8.

Result: company news, requests, booking and reports are collected in one intranet portal.

Technical requirements

  1. News with audience targeting and a form for Marketing
  2. Ctrl+K search, dark theme, mobile menu
  3. A PnP.PowerShell provisioning script and automated tests
  4. A QA audit of all pages before each release: light and dark, 1280 and 390 px
FrameworkSPFx 1.23
