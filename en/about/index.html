01 · Who I am and stack

I am a SharePoint developer. I build web parts on SPFx and portals on SharePoint Online: React, TypeScript, PnPjs, Fluent UI. The data comes from SharePoint lists.

The projects on this site are learning and personal ones: I built them on Microsoft 365 test tenants, so the data in the screenshots is fictional.

I study at BSUIR, Faculty of Computer Design, "Electronic Systems and Technologies", 3rd year. It is an engineering education. In 2026 I completed a SharePoint traineeship.

Open to offers: Minsk or remote.
